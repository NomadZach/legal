# Delete your Pinorama account

App: **Pinorama** · Developer: **NomadZach Studios** · Contact: admin@nomadzachstudios.com

You can delete your Pinorama account at any time. There are two ways.

## 1. In the app (immediate)

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **⚙️ gear** at the top of the screen to open Settings.
3. Tap **"delete my account"** and confirm.

This permanently erases your cloud account and everything stored for it — including your posts and likes — immediately, and signs you out on the phone.

## 2. By email (if you can't sign in)

Email **admin@nomadzachstudios.com** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will action the deletion for you promptly and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your cloud account and everything stored for it (profile, posts, likes, follows, messages, dating data, saved places synced to the cloud, points and referral records).
- **Kept only where the law requires it:** booking records for hotels or other bookings made through Pinorama are deleted with your account, **except records we must keep for bookkeeping and tax law, which are kept only as long as those laws require.**
- **On your phone:** deleting your account signs you out and clears your dating data and conversations from the device. Your saved places, your profile, your notes and your points that live **on the phone** are not deleted with the cloud account — if you want those gone as well, delete the app.
- **No account?** If you never created an account, deleting the app deletes all your Pinorama data — it's the only place it exists.
